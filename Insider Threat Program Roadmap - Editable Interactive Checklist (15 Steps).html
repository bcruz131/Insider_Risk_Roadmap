PHASE I - INITIATION

Triggers for Program Launch

Initiated by specific security incidents, regulatory compliance, or broad risk assessments.

#1: Initial Planning

Primary starting point where baseline objectives of the program are defined.

#2: Identify Stakeholders

Cross-functional collaboration involving C-Suite, Financial, Legal, Security, IT/Cyber, HR, Communications, and Ethics.

📋
PHASE II - PLANNING

#3: Achieve & Sustain Leadership Buy-In

Securing long-term commitment from executive leadership for resources and authority.

#4: Risk Management Process

Identify Critical Assets

Identify Threats

Conduct Vulnerability Assessment

#5: Detailed Project Planning

Translating strategy into actionable project timelines and milestones.

#6: Develop Governance Structure and Policy

Establishing rules, roles, and legal frameworks governing program actions.

#7: Communications, Training, and Awareness

Executing strategy for initial roll-out, periodic training, and ad hoc updates.

🔍
PHASE III - OPERATIONS

#8: Develop Detection Indicators

Identifying specific behaviors or technical signals indicating emerging insider threats. Map to MITRE ATT&CK framework.

#9: Data and Tool Requirements

Determining necessary software and data sources to monitor indicators. SIEM, UEBA, DLP tools.

#10: Data Fusion

Integrating various data streams into a single analytical environment for better visibility and correlation.

#11: Red Team Testing & Validation

Validate detection effectiveness through adversary simulation before full production deployment.

11.1 Develop Test Scenarios: Create realistic insider threat test cases (JIT bypass, credential theft, data exfiltration)
11.2 Execute Red Team Tests: Simulate insider attacks to test detection coverage and response capabilities
11.3 Detection Gap Analysis: Identify missed detections, false positives/negatives, and blind spots
11.4 Remediate Detection Gaps: Tune rules, add missing detections, fix false positives before production

✓ Success Criteria: 90%+ detection rate, <5% false positives, MTTD <15 minutes

#12: Analysis & Incident Management

Operational monitoring and response to insider threat alerts.

12.1 Triage: Initial evaluation and prioritization of alerts
12.2 Internal Investigation: Deep dive analysis and evidence collection
12.3 Reporting: Document findings and notify stakeholders
12.4 External Investigation (if required): Coordinate with law enforcement
12.5 Final Action: Remediation, termination, or legal action
📊
PHASE IV - REPORTING
& FEEDBACK

#13: Management Reporting

Providing formal updates on program performance, detected risks, and mitigation outcomes to executives.

#14: Feedback & Lessons Learned

Analyzing performance to identify gaps, improve detection rules, and refine processes based on real incidents.

Continuous Improvement Cycle
